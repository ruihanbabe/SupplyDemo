# Skyworks SKY13453-385LF official fact summary

## Source boundary

This is an original summary of the public Skyworks datasheet `202830G`, dated 2020-02-11.
The linked PDF is authoritative for all electrical, package, and handling details. This
file intentionally does not reproduce the datasheet.

## Identity and function

Skyworks identifies `SKY13453-385LF` as a single-control SPDT RF switch over the
0.01-to-6.0 GHz range. The device uses a six-pin 1 mm by 1 mm QFN package. In the
HackRF One evidence chain, this supports identification of the specific BOM part; it
does not by itself approve a replacement component.

## Commercial interpretation

The datasheet is a technical source, not a supply commitment. The product information is
subject to change notice in the source document. A customer-facing delivery claim must
instead cite a dated inventory or supplier response and the active internal policy.

## Required follow-up before a substitute recommendation

An alternative needs a separately cited manufacturer document, an engineering compatibility
decision for the requested board revision, and the required commercial approval. An Agent
must return `needs_review` when any of those inputs are missing.

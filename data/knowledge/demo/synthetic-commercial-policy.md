# Northstar RF commercial and delivery policy

> SYNTHETIC DEMO DATA — This is an invented internal policy for a SupplyAgent portfolio
> demonstration. It is not a policy of Skyworks, HackRF, DigiKey, Mouser, element14, or
> any real company.

## Policy identity

Policy version: `NRF-COMM-1.0`

Effective date: 2026-10-20

Owner: Commercial Approval Team

## Delivery communication

Sales may report an externally verified delivery estimate only as an estimate, with its
source date and expiry. Sales must not promise a revised ship date when the linked supply
case is unresolved. Any expected delay of more than seven calendar days requires approval
before a customer-facing update is sent.

## Substitute material

A substitute-material option requires an engineering compatibility record for the relevant
product revision and an approver decision. A supplier product page, a matching package, or
a model-generated comparison is not sufficient evidence by itself.

## Price and quote handling

Price, stock, MOQ, and lead time are volatile. A proposal may compare dated quote snapshots
but must show their source, collection time, currency, and unknown charges. A discount above
five percent or a price change after customer quotation requires approval. No Agent may place
an order, alter a price, or send a customer message.

## Evidence gate

A delivery-risk proposal requires: a scoped CRM conversation or task, a deterministic
supply-risk fact, and this policy version. If any required source conflicts, is stale, or
is missing, the Mission result is `needs_review` rather than `complete`.
